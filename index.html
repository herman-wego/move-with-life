These are the words tattooed on my neck,
and the words I try to live by.

顺其自然

To follow the natural course of things. To stop forcing, stop resisting, and move in harmony with life.

逍遥自在

To live lightly and freely. To be at ease in one’s spirit, unburdened by needless struggle.

So much of human suffering comes from resisting life.

We resist what has already happened. We resist what is happening now. We resist what may happen tomorrow. We cling to plans, expectations, and control, as if life should always unfold according to our wishes. And when it does not, we suffer twice: once from life itself, and once from our argument with it.

But life is not a machine.
It is more like water, more like weather, more like the wind.

It moves in its own rhythm. It changes shape. It cannot be held still for very long.

And wisdom is learning not to force what cannot be forced.

That is the spirit of 顺其自然.

It does not mean giving up. It does not mean weakness or passivity. It means you stop fighting the nature of things. You stop demanding that every season feel like spring, that every path be straight, that every uncertainty be resolved at once.

You can still act. You can still choose. You can still care deeply. But you stop trying to wrestle life into obedience.

A flower does not bloom by force.
A river does not flow by struggle.

Things unfold in their own time.

And when you truly understand that, something begins to soften inside you.

You breathe more deeply.

You hold less tightly.

You stop making a prison out of expectation.

This is where 逍遥自在 begins.

Real freedom is not having complete control over life. Real freedom is being able to remain at ease within it. It is meeting change without panic, uncertainty without collapse, joy without clinging, and pain without becoming trapped inside it.

To live this way is not to escape life.
It is to be fully inside it, but not crushed by it.

There is a quiet strength in that. The strength to adapt. The strength to let go. The strength to trust that not every detour is a mistake, and not every ending is a tragedy.

These words are not just something written on my skin.
They are a reminder.

Do not force what cannot be forced.
Do not cling to what must pass.
Do not lose yourself trying to control the uncontrollable.

Move with life.

Trust its rhythm.

Stay light.

Stay free.

顺其自然，逍遥自在

That is how I try to live.
